Support — Bouncy Cubly

Publisher: Boyann Mornandji, trading as Boyn Studio

Last updated: 23 August 2026

A question, a problem, a bug?

Write to boyn.studio@gmail.com. Every message gets an answer, usually within a few days.

To speed things up, include your device model, your iOS version and, if you have one, your in-game nickname.

I changed phones — where is my progress?

It follows you. Bouncy Cubly creates an anonymous account and automatically saves your coins, gems, skins and scores. Install the game on the new device and launch it: your progress is restored at startup.

If it does not come back, the game most likely started without a connection the first time. Check your connection and relaunch the app. If the problem persists, write to me before playing again: a game played on an empty account can overwrite the saved one.

I paid but received nothing

Open Options → Restore purchases. The Premium Pack is a permanent purchase: it comes back on every device using the same Apple Account, as many times as needed.

Coin packs are consumable: the coins are credited once, at the moment of purchase, and cannot be restored. If coins you paid for never appeared, write to me and attach the receipt Apple sent you.

How do I change my nickname?

Open Options → Nickname. You can change it as often as you like.

My nickname is rejected

Nicknames are filtered automatically to reject offensive content and impersonation. The filter also catches deliberate misspellings, which occasionally rejects a harmless nickname by mistake. Try another wording, or let me know and I will fix the list.

An offensive nickname appears in the ranking

Report it to boyn.studio@gmail.com with the exact nickname. It is removed from the ranking and the account is blocked.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open Options → Legal → Data deletion. Deletion is immediate, permanent and requires no request to me: everything is erased, including your place in the ranking.

The action cannot be undone, and you are asked to confirm it twice before it runs.

The ranking does not load

The ranking and the cloud save are the only features that use the internet. Without a connection they are unavailable, but the game remains fully playable: your progress is kept on the device and syncs as soon as the network is back.

My score did not appear in the ranking

Scores are verified server-side to prevent cheating. A game that is abnormally fast for its score is refused. If you believe a legitimate score was rejected, write to me with the score and roughly when you played.

The game stutters, crashes, or has no sound

  • Fully close the app, then relaunch it.
  • Check that the in-game volume is not at zero in Options, and that your device's Silent mode is off.
  • If it happens again, write to me: device model, iOS version, and what you were doing at the time.

Requests about your personal data

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection: write to boyn.studio@gmail.com. The data processed and your rights are detailed in the privacy policy.
